A pipeline that asks how much it trusts itself.

The Governance Confidence Index turns five kinds of risk into one number, GCI = 1 − R, and the pipeline changes what it does when that number drops. The thesis runs it on five real datasets, from 1,465 to 1,000,000 records.

What these numbers do not show

Risk reduction is measured on the framework's own risk score. In the stable window the reduction is a fixed 15% dampening, so it is built in rather than observed, and no downstream outcome is measured: fraud caught, patients flagged or fake reviews removed. All five datasets also broke their auto-generated SLAs.
