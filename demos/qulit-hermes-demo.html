One poem, many readers.

A classical embedding gives a verse one vector. QuLit keeps every reading at once in a quantum state and lets the reader's context pick one. HERMES then models the reader.

Hazaron khwahishen aisi ke har khwahish pe dam nikle
Ghalib. Desire as slow death, hyperbole of intensity, or being pulled in infinite directions: all three are valid.

In the full pipeline these four angles come from the verse's mBERT embedding. This page sets them directly and runs the real 4-qubit circuit (Hadamard, RY encoding, circular CNOT, RZ/RY, CNOT) in your browser. Trainable parameters are fixed demo values.

Observer-Sensitive Index, live formula

OSI is the spread of meaning under a given reader: √Σ P(meaning | reader) · (meaning − mean)². Here a verse has three readings at fixed positions; weight them the way a reader might. This illustrates the formula, not the trained model.
