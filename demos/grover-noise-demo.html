Model: P(k) = λk sin²((2k+1)θ) + (1 − λk)/N with λ = (1 − ε)g and θ = arcsin(1/√N) (Theorem 4.2). k* solves the optimality equation (Theorem 4.4); Eq. 7 is the asymptotic form (Corollary 4.7). Whole-number k* is the better of the two integers around k*, and GFS = P(k*, ε) / Pideal(kideal). Presets use ε and g from the paper's Table 5.
